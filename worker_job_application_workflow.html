# Worker Application Workflow: From Discovery to Tracking This document maps out the "I Want to Work" journey, specifically focusing on the job discovery and application process. ## 1. Discovery Phase - **Home Dashboard** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_31}}): The worker starts here. The "Jobs" tab is active by default, showing a curated feed of opportunities based on their profile. - **Worker Discovery & Search** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_62}}): If the worker wants to find something specific, they use the search screen. Here they can filter by skill (e.g., Electrician), location (e.g., Mumbai), and view a list of matches. ## 2. Evaluation Phase - **Job Application Screen** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_47}}): After finding an interesting job in the search or feed, the worker taps on it to see full details. This screen shows: - Employer verification status and location. - Specific job requirements (e.g., Welding, Safety Certs). - Detailed salary and duration info. - Contact credit access (to reveal the employer's number if they have credits). ## 3. Action Phase - **Applying**: From the Job Application Screen, the worker taps the primary blue "Apply for This Job" button. This officially submits their profile to the employer. ## 4. Tracking Phase - **My Jobs** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_4}}): Once applied, the worker can track the status of all their active applications here. - **Applied Tab**: Shows newly submitted applications (e.g., "In Review" or "Interviewing"). - **Ongoing Tab**: Shows jobs they have been hired for and are currently working. - **Completed Tab**: Shows past work history and reviews earned. ## 5. Communication - **Messages** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_45}}): If an employer is interested, they may initiate a chat. The worker manages all these professional conversations here.
