# KI App User Workflows ## 1. Worker Workflow (I Want to Work) The journey for a skilled professional looking for work opportunities. ### Onboarding Phase 1. **Splash Screen** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_51}}): Initial app entry. 2. **User Type Selection** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_41}}): Worker role chosen. 3. **OTP Verification** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_72}}): Phone number validation. 4. **Sign-Up Form** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_65}}): Profile creation and skill selection. 5. **Success Screen** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_6}}): Verification completed. ### Daily Engagement Phase 6. **Home Dashboard** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_31}}): Overview of active applications and stats. 7. **Job Search/Discovery** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_62}}): Browsing available job listings. 8. **Job Application** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_47}}): Reviewing specific job details and applying. 9. **My Jobs** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_4}}): Tracking application status (Applied, Ongoing, Completed). 10. **Messages** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_45}}): Chatting with potential employers. 11. **Profile Management** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_61}}): Viewing public profile. 12. **Edit Profile** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_9}}): Updating skills and portfolio. 13. **Subscription/Credits** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_71}}): Managing contact limits and plans. --- ## 2. Employer Workflow (I Want to Hire) The journey for businesses or individuals looking to hire skilled labor. ### Onboarding Phase 1. **Splash Screen** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_51}}): Initial app entry. 2. **User Type Selection** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_7}}): Employer role chosen. 3. **OTP Verification** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_66}}): Phone number validation. 4. **Sign-Up Form** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_55}}): Company profile setup. 5. **Success Screen** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_68}}): Business verification completed. ### Hiring Phase 6. **Home Dashboard** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_11}}): Overview of active posts and metrics. 7. **Post a Job** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_69}}): Creating new job requirements. 8. **Worker Discovery** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_62}}): Searching for specific skills or workers. 9. **Applicant Management** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_13}}): Reviewing, shortlisting, and hiring candidates. 10. **Messages** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_70}}): Communicating with applicants. 11. **Edit Profile** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_42}}): Updating company details and docs. 12. **Subscription/Credits** ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_19}}): Managing business plans and contact credits.
